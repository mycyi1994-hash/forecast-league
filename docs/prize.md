# Daily prize: ended before it started

The daily prize (1 USDT to the best win rate, announced for 21 November to 9 December 2026) was cancelled on
20 November 2026, before its first day. No prize was ever paid and none will be. Forecast League has no prize,
reward or payout of any kind: tickets, cards, badges, honors and records have no monetary value.

Why: a cash prize for calling prices up or down could be read as a promised return, and this is a free game.
The records, streaks, badges and honors stay exactly as they are.
